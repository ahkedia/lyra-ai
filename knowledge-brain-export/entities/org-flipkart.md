# Flipkart

## Overview
Flipkart is India's leading e-commerce conglomerate (a subsidiary of Walmart Inc.), operating massive consumer platforms, logistics networks, and financial service rails across the Indian subcontinent.

## Relationship to Akash Kedia
Akash served as a Senior Product Manager / Product Leader within Flipkart Pay and Financial Services. Notably, he conceptualized, built, and launched **Flipkart Pay Later**, pioneering scalable consumer credit and Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) infrastructure that processed millions of transactions and served as a foundational payments rail for the platform.
