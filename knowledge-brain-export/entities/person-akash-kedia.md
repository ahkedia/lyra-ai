# Akash Kedia

## Executive Summary
Akash Kedia is a senior product leader and executive in the global financial technology sector. He has led product organizations of over 100 people across top European and Indian fintech and technology institutions, including **N26**, **Flipkart**, **Trade Republic**, and **CheQ**. He specializes in high-throughput payments processing, consumer and SME credit & lending underwriting, neo-banking, retail investment infrastructure, and autonomous AI agents.

## Verified Career History & Employers
- **N26** (`org-n26`): Senior product leadership in Berlin, Germany. Led major banking, payments, and expansion product areas within Europe's premier mobile bank.
- **Trade Republic** (`org-trade-republic`): Product leadership within Europe's largest savings and neo-broker platform, overseeing wealth, investing, and core product scaling.
- **CheQ** (`org-cheq`): Senior executive product leader building credit management, credit card bill payments, and lending solutions for consumers.
- **Flipkart** (`org-flipkart`): Product leader within Flipkart Pay / Financial Services, spearheading the creation and scaling of **Flipkart Pay Later** (buy now, pay later) and consumer credit infrastructure.

## Personal AI & Systems
